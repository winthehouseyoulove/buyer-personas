Reframe

FHA buyers earn more than you'd think

FHA buyers' median income ($103k) is higher than conventional buyers' ($91k). They choose FHA for credit (median 660), not income. The story is "good income, bruised credit, here's your path."

Timely

Rates didn't stop them — make that the hook

Median rate locked was ~6.9%, with buyers closing up to 8.5%. Your audience acts in a high-rate market. "Buy now, refinance later," buydowns, and seller credits are high-intent topics.

Positioning

You're a national, affordable-market channel

Buyers span 25 states, led by TX & NC, median price $312k — well below coastal prices. Don't make California-priced content; show $250k–$400k homes.
